Highlights

- Open-source Python package for dipole searches in binary-labelled sky catalogues
- Joint monopole-dipole least squares removes footprint-induced false dipoles
- Vectorised label permutation calibrates the free-axis test in seconds
- Injection-recovery quantifies what a given catalogue can and cannot detect
- Galaxy Zoo 1: no spin dipole; omitting the monopole gives a spurious 5-sigma axis
